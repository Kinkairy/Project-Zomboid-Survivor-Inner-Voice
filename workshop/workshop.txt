[quote]角色会根据自己的饥饿、口渴、疲劳、疼痛、情绪和其他当前状态，用简短直白的头顶文字自言自语。[/quote]

[h1]状态心声[/h1]

[list]
[*]角色能够直接感受到的主要原版状态，也包含吃饱等正面状态。
[*]同一种状态和严重程度有多句不同说法，减少重复感。
[*]状态出现、加重、减轻和完全恢复时，都可能出现对应台词。
[*]进入世界后会先等待一段时间，不会刚加载完成就立刻刷出台词。
[*]原版状态标题和说明固定使用小号深灰色 <内容> 头顶提示；原创角色心声不加括号，并在句末显示对应的原版 Moodle 图标。
[/list]

[h1]严重程度与颜色[/h1]

[list]
[*]负面状态从淡红逐渐变为深红，状态越严重，台词也越明确。
[*]正面状态从淡绿逐渐变为深绿；负面状态完全恢复时会显示浅绿色恢复台词。
[*]颜色沿用游戏当前设置中的原版正面与负面高亮色。
[/list]

[h1]沙盒选项[/h1]

[list]
[*]角色心声字体大小可选小、中、大、特大，默认中；原版状态提示固定为小号。
[*]“显示原版状态提示”默认开启；关闭后只显示角色心声。
[*]台词频率可选很少、较少、普通、频繁、很频繁。
[*]实际出现时间会随机变化，不会按固定秒数重复。
[*]所有频率都只保留每种状态的最新变化，不会积压并补播过时症状。
[/list]

[h1]兼容性[/h1]

支持 Project Zomboid Build 42.20、单人游戏、多人游戏和专用服务器；无前置依赖，可加入现有存档。

[h1]Survivor Inner Voice[/h1]

[quote]Your character reacts to hunger, thirst, fatigue, pain, mood, and other current conditions with short, readable overhead thoughts.[/quote]

[h1]State Reactions[/h1]

[list]
[*]Covers the major vanilla conditions a character can directly feel, including positive states such as being well fed.
[*]Each state and severity has multiple lines to reduce repetition.
[*]Lines can appear when a state begins, worsens, improves, or fully clears.
[*]A short randomized delay after entering the world prevents immediate message spam.
[*]Vanilla state titles and descriptions use a fixed Small dark-gray <text> overhead prompt; original thoughts are unwrapped and leave a gap before the matching vanilla Moodle icon.
[/list]

[h1]Severity and Color[/h1]

[list]
[*]Negative states progress from pale red to deep red, with clearer wording at higher severity.
[*]Positive states progress from pale green to deep green; full recovery from a negative state uses a pale-green recovery line.
[*]Colors follow the current vanilla positive and negative highlight-color settings.
[/list]

[h1]Sandbox Options[/h1]

[list]
[*]Inner-voice font size offers Small, Medium, Large, or Massive and defaults to Medium; vanilla state prompts stay Small.
[*]Show vanilla state prompts is enabled by default; disable it to show only character thoughts.
[*]Frequency offers Very Rare, Rare, Normal, Frequent, or Very Frequent.
[*]Actual timing is randomized instead of repeating at fixed intervals.
[*]Every frequency keeps only the latest change per state, without building up and replaying outdated symptoms.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

Supports Project Zomboid Build 42.20, singleplayer, multiplayer, and dedicated servers. No dependencies; safe to add to an existing save.
